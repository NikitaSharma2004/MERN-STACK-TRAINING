CHEMISTRY IN EVERYDAY LIFE

:-INTRODUCTION-:

Chemistry is all Around us .From the food we eat to the air we breathe,chemical reactions are happening everywhere . In this Webpage ,we will Explore some common chemical reactions and their importance in our daily lives.

1. Rusting of iron

Rusting occcur when iron reacts with oxygen and moisture present in the air.It forms a reddish-brown substnce called rust.

Equation:-


	4Fe + 3O2 = 2Fe2O3

2.Reaction of Baking Soda and Vinegar

When baking soda reacts with vinegar ,carbon dioxide gas is released.The bubbles produced during the reaction are due to this gas.

Equation:-


	NaHCO3 + CH3COOH = CH3COONa + H2O + CO2

3.Photosynthesis

Green plants use carbon dioxide and water in the presensce of sunlight to produce glucose and oxygen. The process is called Photosynthesis.

Equation:-


	6CO2 + 6H2O = C6H12O6 + 6O2

4.Combustion of Methane

Methane burns in oxygen to produce carbon dioxide, water and energy. This reaction is an example of a combustion reaction.

Equation:-


CH4 + 2O2 → CO2 + 2H2O + Energy

"Did You Know ?"
